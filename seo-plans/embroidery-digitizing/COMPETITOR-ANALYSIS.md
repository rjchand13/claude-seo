## Market norms

- **Price:** $1 to $1.50 per 1,000 stitches is standard, with $6 to $10 minimums. A typical left-chest logo (about 6,000 to 10,000 stitches) costs $8 to $15.
- **Speed:** 2 to 12 hours normal; rush costs $5 to $10 extra.
- **Standard promises:** free edits, all formats, first design free or discounted.

None of these is a differentiator anymore. They are the minimum a new site must match.

## Patterns across competitors

| Area | What they do | Gap for you |

| Content | Similar service pages with the same keyword lists | Original, detailed pages with real examples |

| Pricing | Mixed: some public, some "get a quote" | Interactive calculator plus a public table |
| Formats and machines | Lists of file extensions | A real guide hub: which machine uses which format, and why |
| Reviews | Testimonials on their own site | Third-party reviews (Trustpilot, Google) |
| Video | Rare | Stitch-out videos on every service page |

## Keyword gaps worth targeting first

These are lower competition and high intent. Check volumes before building.

1. Format conversion pages: "convert logo to DST", "JPG to PES file", "PNG to embroidery file".
2. Machine-specific pages: "embroidery file for Brother PE800", "Tajima DST digitizing", "Janome JEF file".
3. Hard techniques: "small text digitizing", "3D puff cap digitizing", "chenille patch digitizing".
4. Comparisons: "DST vs PES", "auto digitizing vs manual digitizing", "Wilcom vs Hatch".
5. Problems: "why does my embroidery pucker", "thread breaks on logo", "fix a bad digitized file". Searchers with a broken file are ready to pay for a re-digitize.
